    Why iPhone photos won't open on Windows

    Since iOS 11 the iPhone camera saves HEIC, which is about half the size of JPEG at similar quality. Windows doesn't include a HEIC codec by default, and many upload forms reject it. A HEIC to JPG converter online is the quickest iPhone photo format fix, with no software to download.

    Why images save as WebP

    Websites serve WebP because it's 25–35% smaller than JPEG. When you save one, older editors and office tools can't open it. Use the WebP to PNG converter free above to get a universal file.
